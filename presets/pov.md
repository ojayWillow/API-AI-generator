# Preset: pov

First-person "POV: you ..." clips for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

- aspect_ratio: 9:16
- still resolution: 1K draft, 2K final
- image model: nano-banana-2
- video model: pro, 10 s
- reference images: none (add URLs here to lock a look)

## Locked prompt fragment (prepend to every still prompt)

```
first-person POV shot, hands visible at the bottom of the frame, eye-level,
35mm lens, natural light, cinematic color grade, photorealistic, vertical 9:16,
no text, no watermark, no logo
```

## Locked motion fragment (prepend to every video prompt)

```
subtle handheld sway, slow forward drift, hands shift slightly, keep the
first-person point of view, no camera cuts
```

## Example

Still: `first-person POV shot, hands visible ... waking up in a marble Roman
villa bedroom at dawn, silk sheets, a senator's toga folded on a chair, sunlight
through columns`

Video: `subtle handheld sway ... curtains move in the breeze, dust motes in the
light`
